with Bounds

Calculating with Bounds Q1
A rectangle has length 12 cm and width 8 cm, both to the nearest cm.

Find the maximum possible area.
12.5 × 8.5 = 106.25 cm²
Calculating with Bounds Q2
A rectangle has length 12 cm and width 8 cm, both to the nearest cm.

Find the minimum possible area.
11.5 × 7.5 = 86.25 cm²
Calculating with Bounds Q3
Two parcels weigh 3.4 kg and 2.7 kg, both to 1 d.p.

Find the maximum total weight.
3.45 + 2.75 = 6.2 kg
Calculating with Bounds Q4
A piece of rope is 50 m long (nearest metre). A piece 18 m (nearest metre) is cut off.

Find the minimum length remaining.
49.5 − 18.5 = 31 m
Calculating with Bounds Q5
