Significant Figures
Rounding
aRound 47 653 to 3 s.f.
bRound 0.003 0824 to 3 s.f.
Answers
a) 47 700    b) 0.003 08
a) 4, 7, 6 are the first 3 s.f. → round up.
b) Leading zeros don't count — 3, 0, 8 are the first 3 s.f.

Answers
a) 5.29 × 10⁻⁴    b) 3.61 × 10⁵

b) 36.1 is not between 1 and 10 — divide by 10, increase power by 1.

Upper & Lower Bounds
Bounds
A parcel weighs 4.6 kg, measured to the nearest tenth of a kg.
State the upper and lower bounds.
Answer
4.55 ≤ w < 4.65
Nearest tenth → half unit = 0.05.
Lower = 4.6 − 0.05 = 4.55  |  Upper = 4.6 + 0.05 = 4.65

Percentage Error
% Error
A student estimates √50 to be 7.0.
The exact value is 7.071…
Find the percentage error to 3 s.f.
Use the formula from your booklet.
Answer
1.00 %
|7.0 − 7.071…| ÷ 7.071… × 100 = 0.071… ÷ 7.071… × 100 ≈ 1.00%
