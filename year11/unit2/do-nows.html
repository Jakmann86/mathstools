% Error
A student estimates √50 as 7.0. The exact value is 7.071… Find the percentage error to 3 s.f.
% error = |approx − exact| ÷ exact × 100
Answer
1.00%
|7.0 − 7.071…| ÷ 7.071… × 100 = 0.071… ÷ 7.071… × 100 ≈ 1.00%

A bag of rice is labelled 500 g. When weighed, it measures 483 g. Find the percentage error to 3 s.f.
% error = |VA − VE| ÷ VE × 100   (VE = exact value)
Answer
3.40%
