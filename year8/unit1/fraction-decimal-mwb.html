Big Idea
Decimals and fractions are different ways of writing the same number. To combine them, we convert so they speak the same language.
📚 Key Vocabulary
Two conversion methods
Decimal → Fraction
Read the place value, then simplify.
"75 hundredths" → simplify by GCD
Fraction → Decimal
Divide numerator by denominator.
3 ÷ 4 = 0.75
Stem sentence: "To combine a decimal and a fraction, I convert them to the same form — either both decimals or both fractions."
